BBQ . HEAT . FEAST. Open Fire Food Festival is back.

The Open Fire Food
Festival is a
fiery celebration
of bold flavors, cultural diversity,
and the primal
art of cooking
over open flames.

Inspired by Steve Harvey’s passion for fire-grilled
food, this two-day festival
brings together the
world’s top celebrity BBQ
chefs and pitmasters
for a one-of-a-kind
culinary experience.

From sizzling grills and smoky bites
to great tunes and unbeatable
vibes, OFFF is more than just food –
it’s a feast for the senses and a
celebration of
community.

At the heart of the festival
are the buzzing open
fire backyards, where
live-fire cooking, hands-on experiences, and
storytelling collide.

Whether you’re a hardcore grill master, a curious foodie, or a family looking for fun, OFFF offers something unforgettable for everyone.
